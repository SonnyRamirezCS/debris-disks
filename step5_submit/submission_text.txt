I fit a line, a parabola, and a blackbody to the 8 photometry points for SSTSL2 J012412.12-741735.2. The line fits poorly. Its residuals are almost as large as the data. The parabola follows the points better but goes negative between about 8 and 24 microns, which isn't physical. The blackbody fit gives T = 3573 K, close to the dataset's Teff of 3493 K, so it's the only model that matches the physics. Since our fluxes are in Janskys (per frequency), I used the per frequency Planck function instead of the per wavelength version from Canvas, which gave temperatures that were way too low. The 24 micron point falls slightly below the blackbody curve, so this star shows no infrared excess and no sign of a debris disk.
